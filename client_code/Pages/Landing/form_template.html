Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every program, lab, drill, exam and certificate is free for now. There are no ads and we don’t sell your data. If we ever add paid extras, what you’ve earned stays yours.

Who is it for?

Software engineers moving into AI work, data scientists and ML engineers moving to LLM products, and tech leads who review AI designs. You should be comfortable reading code; the programs teach systems thinking, not one framework.

Will this get me a $200k job?

No course can promise that, and you should be wary of any that does. What we can do is teach the skills interviewers and senior engineers actually probe: retrieval quality, agent control, evaluation, security and cost. The capstones are written like system-design interview questions.

How long does it take?

Each module is about an hour with drills and the challenge; each program is a few evenings. With five hours a week, all seven programs take roughly three months. Make a plan after you sign up and it’ll give you dates.

Where does the content come from?

From primary sources: research papers, engineering write-ups from the teams that built these systems, official docs, standards like the OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications, and court and news records for the incidents. Every module lists its sources, and the Library links to the originals.

Is the certificate accredited?

No. It’s issued by AI Expert on its own authority, says exactly what you completed, and can be verified by anyone with the link. Treat it as evidence of work, not a licence.

Which models and frameworks do you teach?

The ideas that outlast them. Examples mention real tools (BM25, rerankers, LangGraph, MCP, vLLM, OpenTelemetry), but the drills and challenges test the engineering judgement that transfers between providers.
